AI providers & free models

LectureMind can use free-tier APIs through their official endpoints. OpenRouter provides a single OpenAI-compatible API with a live catalog of currently free models; Gemini also has a free API tier, and Groq offers a free developer tier with rate limits. Free availability and limits can change.

OpenRouter — Free Models

Live catalog of free models from multiple providers.

Google Gemini — Free Tier

Direct Gemini API with its current free-tier limits.

Groq — Free Tier

Fast models through Groq's OpenAI-compatible API.

DeepSeek v4.1 Flash TokenHarbor

Free DeepSeek v4.1 Flash via TokenHarbor's OpenAI-compatible API.

DeepSeek v4.1 Flash NVIDIA NIM

DeepSeek v4.1 Flash via NVIDIA's integrate.api.nvidia.com endpoint.

DeepSeek v4 Pro Official

DeepSeek's own API, official endpoint.
